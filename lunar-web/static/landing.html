Scientific assumptions and limitations

These values are sampled astronomical Sun/Earth azimuth and elevation angles returned by JPL Horizons for a lunar surface observer at elevation 0 km. Time above 0° is integrated with linear interpolation at the sample cadence. This is a smooth-horizon visibility assessment, not terrain-aware sunlight analysis. Local topography may block the Sun or Earth. Communication link budgets, relay spacecraft, shadow persistence, thermal constraints, surface roughness, regolith, and safe landing ellipses are not evaluated. The charts do not establish a viable landing site or operational communications coverage.
